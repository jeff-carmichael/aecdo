# Mark up the PDF

Add clouds, callouts, and notes that Bluebeam and Acrobat open as normal markups.

## Add a markup

- Press <kbd>M</kbd> on what's selected, or
- hold <kbd>Shift</kbd> and drag a box on the PDF, or
- ask the AI: "cloud every door missing from the schedule".

Markups save as you add them, to a copy beside your PDF: `plans-markups.pdf`. Your original isn't changed.

## Sets

Keep markups for different purposes apart, such as QA or code review. The bar under the PDF shows the **Current PDF version**: **Original** or one of your markup sets. Pick one from its menu to see it, or press **+ New** and pick a suggested name (Code review, QA, Punch list…) or type your own. Each set is its own copy, like `plans-markups-qa.pdf`; hover the menu to see which file is showing. New markups go to the last set you picked.

## Undo and history

Press <kbd>m</kbd> to list the set's markups:

- <kbd>Enter</kbd> goes to one, <kbd>x</kbd> deletes it, <kbd>u</kbd> undoes.
- <kbd>h</kbd> shows the history. Pick a step to go back to it.

Markups land where the data says things are. Check them on the PDF.
